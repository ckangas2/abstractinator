# Tag agreement with PubTator3 (2026-11-11)

54 searches, 1853 PubMed records. Our `primary_virus` and `primary_bacteria` are compared with the species PubTator3 annotates in the same title and abstract, mapped onto our categories through NCBI Taxonomy lineages. Generated by `deploy/benchmark_pubtator.py`.

How to read this:

- PubTator3 is itself a model, so every figure is **agreement**, not accuracy.
- **Tag support** is the main figure: how often a tag we assign is backed by a PubTator3 species annotation in the same record. **Agreement** is narrower: it leaves out records where PubTator3 annotated no organism of that kind (it often labels a virus only as a disease), so it is always the higher number.
- Brackets are 95% confidence intervals (Wilson).
- Only records with a PMID are compared, and those are the ones where curated MeSH terms can override our text matching. Grants, trials and preprints, which rely on text matching alone, are not covered; nor are cell types (PubTator3 has none).
